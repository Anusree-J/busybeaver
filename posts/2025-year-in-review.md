**2025 felt like a year where it seemed like nothing happened — when in reality, a lot did.** Maybe it didn't _feel_ big because I was being held up by the good pills (truly doing God's work). We're jumping right in.

January hit me too fast. I wasn't rested enough, and I walked into the year already burnt out from the endless trips, plans, and general chaos of 2024. Looking back, I was unhappy at work — and from experience, that's a huge trigger for me. I had taken on way too many random tasks, and honestly I'm surprised I didn't break earlier. New faces, new orgs, new countries — but the same constant running. Running for what? I don't think I had an answer.

In hindsight, I was struggling with two things:

1. I knew I didn't want to do what I was doing — but I hadn't figured out _what else_.

2. I didn't (or couldn't) say no. I took on everything like a human dumpster for responsibility.


All of this came out as anger. Not subtle anger — volcanic. I felt like throwing things, yelling, slapping someone, even jumping off my balcony. I was snapping at my partner over trivial things while he was going through his own rough patch. I was highly strung.

Late March was my lowest point I even bought a Calm subscription.

**The best decision I made all year was getting help.**

It changed everything. Slowly, not magically. With side effects — increased hunger, gut issues — but worth every bit. I became lighter. Less "AHHHHHHHH." Thought spirals softened. I felt ideas again, I started reading again, my mind finally had space.

By Q2, I was clearer. Still unsure about quitting, but confidence and doubt were both growing. More trips came — unsurprisingly in Asia — including one that was slightly too long, but I rode in a Cambodian tuk-tuk, tried bubble tea for the first time, ate unhealthy amounts of tofu — joy.

Then Singapore happened. I had been looking forward to it because it's one of the places I dream about living in while building my startup. I loved it — public transport, structure, safety, efficiency. My taste has shifted too — cheap Chinese to less-cheap Chinese — growth. The zoo sparked childlike joy.

Then Switzerland — Geneva, you beauty. Overpriced and absolutely worth it. I bathed in the fountain, walked by the lake, and I took a step that scared me — I **asked** for advice. I'll hold those never-ending summer days close.

My birthday was warm and full of laughter. Q3 and Q4 were steadier — friends, love, building things, embracing uncertainty. I applied to YC and got rejected — but I APPLIED, and I'm proud. My home finally feels like a sanctuary. I worked out (although not consistently) for the first time in my life. December ended peacefully with family and books — a closing exhale.

**2026 will be better.**

The best year yet — success, love, health, joy, prosperity. I'm manifesting all of it.
