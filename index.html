About

Hi, I'm glad you're here. This is a small corner of the internet where I keep track of what I'm curious about.

Lately I keep circling back to the same questions—how do we work, and how do our bodies work? What actually causes illness, and could we one day cure all disease? And underneath all of it: how do we become the best versions of ourselves, and how do we take care of our minds along the way?

I don't have the answers yet. But I like sitting with the questions, and this page is where I do it out loud.
